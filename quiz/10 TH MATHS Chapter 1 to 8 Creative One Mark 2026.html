2. The \( p^{th} \) term of an A.P. is \( \frac{3p-1}{6} \). The sum of the first \( n \) terms of the A.P. is:
Correct Answer: (2)

Last term \( l = t_n = \frac{3n-1}{6} \).

\( = \frac{n}{2}\left(\frac{2 + 3n - 1}{6}\right) = \frac{n(3n+1)}{12} \).

1. \( \frac{\sqrt{1-\sin^2\theta}}{\sin\theta} = ? \)
Correct Answer: (1)

\( \tan^2\theta + \cot^2\theta + 2\tan\theta\cot\theta = 4 \)
